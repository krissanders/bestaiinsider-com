Audit my site How it works →
The audits we run on every domain:
  • Which of the 22 known AI bots (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Bytespider, CCBot, etc.) can read your site — per-bot, line by line
  • Whether Cloudflare's AI-bot defaults are silently blocking AI search at the edge
  • Whether your homepage is an empty SPA shell to crawlers that don't run JavaScript
  • Schema.org / JSON-LD coverage — the entity grounding LLMs need to cite you
  • llms.txt presence, sitemap coverage, meta-robots flags including noai / noimageai
  • Whether your brand surfaces in real LLM answers — same query, fanned across Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Gemini
  • How your numbers compare to your competitors, side by side
